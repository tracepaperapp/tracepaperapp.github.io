Modeling in tracepaper

This clip shows the opening steps of the guided tutorial in Tracepaper.

You begin by defining the data objects: the Document (state), the Domain Events (state transitions), and the Commands (intent).

Next you write the behavior specification: “given this command goes in, I expect this event to come out, and the document to look like this.”

Finally, you model the behavior that ties those pieces together.

The instructions shown in the lower-left come from the tutorial. Pause the video if it moves too fast, or run the tutorial yourself — it’s the best way to get a feel for Tracepaper.
