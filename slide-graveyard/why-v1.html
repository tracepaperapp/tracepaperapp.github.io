When you want to launch a SaaS product, clients expect it to be available anywhere, anytime. It has to be robust and scalable from day one.

But as a part-time founder, with no time and no money, that feels impossible. The expectations are simply out of reach.

That’s why serverless technology, like AWS, feels like the perfect solution. It gives you global scale without the heavy upfront cost.

But serverless brings a new problem: a skewed signal-to-noise ratio. You end up with more lines of infrastructure code than actual business logic.

What I really want is to focus on the functional domain — the part that actually delivers value — without drowning in the complexity of infrastructure.

And that’s exactly the problem Tracepaper is built to solve. It bridges the gap between what SaaS demands and what founders can realistically build.
