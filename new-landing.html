How it works

Three steps. Full ownership.

You design the behavior. Tracepaper handles the infrastructure. Your code, your repo, your AWS account.

01

Model your domain

Define commands, events, aggregates, and views in the Tracepaper Modeler — a desktop IDE built for domain-driven design. Instant validation keeps your model consistent as you work.

02

Build & verify

Trigger a build. The Tracepaper Build Agent generates production-ready code and runs behavior tests derived from your specifications — passing or failing in seconds.

03

Deploy to your AWS

Generated code lands in your repo. Your AWS pipeline deploys via CloudFormation. Scenarios act as quality gates — promoting staging to production only when everything passes.

Is it for you?

Tracepaper is opinionated — by design

It's not for everyone, and that's the point.

🟢 Great fit

  • You think in domains, not in frameworks
  • You want production-grade serverless without the boilerplate
  • You value owning your infrastructure and code
  • You appreciate short feedback loops over long deploy cycles
  • You're building event-driven systems on AWS

🔴 Not the right tool

  • You want pixel-perfect UI design — Tracepaper generates backends, not frontends
  • You prefer drag-and-drop over modeling behavior
  • You're not deploying to AWS
  • You want a hosted platform that runs your app for you
  • You're looking for a no-code solution
